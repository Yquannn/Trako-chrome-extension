Meet Trako Order Exporter. This guide shows Shopee Philippines.

Install Trako from the Chrome Web Store. Sign in normally and open My Purchases. Trako never asks for your password.

Load the orders you want: scroll and choose Load More when available. Trako reads only orders currently loaded.

Choose Export orders to CSV in Trako's on-page panel. It checks each loaded order and exports completed orders only. Progress and skipped-product reasons stay visible. Cancel, retry, or scan again any time.

When the scan finishes, save the CSV and open it in a spreadsheet. Trako includes order details visible on the page. Some dates or fields may be blank if Shopee does not show them in history.

Your order data stays in your browser and is never sent to Trako servers. Export your own history. Trako Order Exporter.
